Gulaab Jamun

Ingredients

  • Milk powder
  • Maida
  • Baking soda
  • Milk
  • Sugar
  • Water
  • Cardamom
  • Oil or Ghee

Recipe

  1. Mix milk powder, maida, baking soda and milk to make soft dough..
  2. Make small smooth balls.
  3. Fry balls on low flame till golden brown.
  4. Prepare sugar syrup using sugar, water and cardamom.
  5. Put fried balls in warm syrup and leave for some time.

Rasmalai 2.0

Ingredients

  • Full cream milk
  • Lemon juice or vinegar
  • Sugar
  • Water
  • Cardamom powder
  • Saffron
  • Dry fruits

Recipe

  1. Boil milk, add lemon juice and make chenna.
  2. Knead chenna and make small flat balls.
  3. Boil balls in sugar water till soft.
  4. Boil milk with sugar, cardamom and saffron
  5. Put balls in milk, cool and serve.

Shri khand

Ingredients

  • Thick curd
  • Powdered sugar
  • Cardamom powder
  • Saffron
  • Dry fruits

Recipe

  1. Tie curd in cloth and remove water.
  2. Take thick curd and beat till smooth.
  3. Add powdered sugar and mix well.
  4. Add cardamom, saffron and dry fruits.
  5. Chill in fridge and serve cold.

Kaju katli

Ingredients

  • Sugar
  • Ghee
  • Cashews

Recipe

  1. Grind cashews into fine powder.
  2. Boil sugar with little water to make syrup.
  3. Add cashew powder and mix well.
  4. Cook till dough forms and roll flat.
  5. Cut into pieces and let it cool.
